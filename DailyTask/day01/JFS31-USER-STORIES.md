# JFS-31 Campus Placement & Internship Portal

## User Stories

### FR1 — Student Profile & Resume

**User Story:**

As a Student, I want to maintain my academic profile and upload my resume so that my placement information is available to recruiters.

**Story Points:** 5

---

### FR2 — Company Drive

**User Story:**

As a Placement Officer, I want to create a company placement drive with eligibility criteria so that eligible students can participate.

**Story Points:** 5

---

### FR3 — Eligibility

**User Story:**

As a Student, I want to see only the placement drives for which I am eligible so that I can apply to relevant opportunities.

**Story Points:** 8

---

### FR4 — Application

**User Story:**

As a Student, I want to apply for an eligible placement drive before its deadline so that my application can be considered.

**Story Points:** 5

---

### FR5 — Round Results

**User Story:**

As a Recruiter or Placement Officer, I want to upload round-wise results so that student shortlisting can be tracked throughout the recruitment process.

**Story Points:** 8

---

### FR6 — Offer Policy

**User Story:**

As a Placement Officer, I want the system to enforce the offer policy so that students receive offers according to placement rules.

**Story Points:** 8

---

### FR7 — Training

**User Story:**

As a Student, I want to register for placement training sessions so that I can prepare for recruitment drives.

**Story Points:** 3

---

### FR8 — Statistics

**User Story:**

As a Placement Officer, I want to view branch-wise placement statistics so that I can monitor placement performance.

**Story Points:** 5

---
